# Snare

Pathfinder 2e traps for Foundry VTT, without the bookkeeping.

- **Secret detection from exploration activities.** Players pick what their character does while exploring (from the Triad tray or the token HUD). Traps with no minimum proficiency give everyone a check; the rest only check for characters who are Searching with the right Perception rank. The check happens in a detection zone around the trigger, before anyone steps on it, and a token that finds the trap stops at the edge.
- **The trigger is not where the attack comes from.** Paint the pressure plate, then place the origin anywhere: a wall slot, the ceiling, a statue.
- **Chained effects.** Projectile strikes against AC, bursts with saves, conditions, and terrain changes, run in order. With [Pyre](https://github.com/daember-rpg/pyre) active, projectiles and bursts are animated.
- **Terrain that knows who it affects.** Slippery ground (Balance), hazardous ground, and difficult or greater difficult terrain with real movement cost. Exempt specific tokens, whole sides, or creatures with a trait (a frost elemental walks on its own ice).
- **Disable and rearm.** Disable checks with minimum ranks and multiple successes, critical failures that set the trap off, one-use, manual or self-rearming traps.
- **Start from the PF2e hazards compendium** or your own library.

## Using it

1. As GM, open Snare from the token controls (diamond icon) or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>.
2. Create a trap, then paint its trigger squares on the map. Place the origin and add effects.
3. Players set their exploration activity. Move tokens as usual: Snare rolls, stops tokens and fires the trap.
